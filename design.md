# Design Document — Swasthya-Setu

## 1. Overview
This document defines the UI/UX design for the Swasthya-Setu platform.  
The goal is to create a calm, trustworthy, and ethical healthcare interface suitable for government-aligned digital health systems.

This document focuses on **design decisions**, not implementation details.

---

## 2. Design Principles

- Trust-first design
- Calm, non-alarming visuals
- Accessibility and clarity
- Government-style neutrality
- AI transparency (no black-box feel)

---

## 3. Target Users

- Patients (non-technical, healthcare-seeking users)
- Doctors / Practitioners
- Diagnostic staff (indirect interaction)

## 5. Information Architecture

### Patient Dashboard
- Welcome & context
- Government health awareness
- Diagnostic flow explanation
- Guidance & next steps
- Trust & disclaimers

### Navigation
- Sidebar-based navigation
- Clear section labeling
- No hidden actions

---

## 6. Key Screens

### 6.1 Patient Dashboard (Home)

**Purpose**
- Reassure patients
- Explain system flow
- Build trust before interaction

**Sections**
1. Welcome & Context
2. Government Health Initiatives
3. How the System Works
4. Quick Guidance
5. Disclaimer & Trust

---

## 7. Component Design

### Reusable Components
- Card
- Badge
- Alert (info)
- Separator

### Styling
- Neutral colors (blue, white, green)
- No aggressive contrasts
- Mobile-responsive layouts

---

## 8. Interaction & States

- No auto-playing animations
- Hover effects only where necessary
- Clear empty states
- Informational over actionable UI

---

## 9. AI Transparency in UI

- AI is positioned as **assistive**
- Doctor oversight is always visible
- No autonomous claims
- Advisory disclaimers included

---

## 10. Accessibility Considerations

- Readable font sizes
- High contrast text
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Simple language

---

## 11. Non-Goals

This design explicitly does NOT include:
- Data analytics dashboards
- Raw medical data visualization
- Editable medical records
- AI confidence scores

---

## 12. Future Design Scope

- Multilingual support
- Accessibility enhancements
- Expanded patient education modules

(Future scope is intentionally high-level.)
